📱 Using on Phone / Bluefy

  1. Install the free Bluefy app from the iOS App Store.
  2. Open this GitHub Pages URL in Bluefy.
  3. Click Connect to StickS3.
  4. Select StickS3-FindMy.
  5. Tap Play Sound to make your M5StickS3 ring!
  6. Press the front button on StickS3 or tap Stop Sound to silence.
